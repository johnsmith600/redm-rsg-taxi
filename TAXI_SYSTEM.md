# RSG Taxi System - Anyone Can Drive

## 🚖 **How to Become a Taxi Driver**

**Anyone can become a taxi driver!** No special job required.

### **Step 1: Open Taxi Menu**
- Type `/taxi` in chat
- This opens the taxi driver menu for everyone

### **Step 2: Spawn a Taxi Vehicle**
- Click "Spawn Vehicle" in the menu
- Choose from available taxi vehicles:
  - Horse-drawn buggy
  - Simple cart  
  - Covered wagon
  - Stagecoach

### **Step 3: Start Working**
- Once you have a taxi vehicle, click "Start Work"
- You're now a taxi driver and can accept passengers!

### **Step 4: Accept Rides**
- Players can call taxis using `/taxi` as passengers
- You'll get notifications for ride requests
- Accept rides to earn money

## 🎯 **Key Features**

- **No Job Restrictions**: Anyone can drive a taxi
- **Vehicle-Based System**: Just spawn a taxi vehicle to become a driver
- **Automatic Detection**: System detects when you're in a taxi vehicle
- **Easy Access**: Simple `/taxi` command for everyone
- **Multiple Vehicles**: Choose from various period-appropriate vehicles

## 🚗 **Available Taxi Vehicles**

1. **buggy01** - Horse-drawn buggy (fast, 1-2 passengers)
2. **cart01** - Simple cart (basic, 1-2 passengers)  
3. **wagon02** - Covered wagon (comfortable, 2-4 passengers)
4. **coach2** - Stagecoach (premium, 4-6 passengers)

## 💰 **Earning Money**

- **Base Fare**: Starting fee for each ride
- **Distance Fare**: Payment per mile traveled
- **Time Fare**: Payment for time spent driving
- **Tips**: Passengers can tip for good service
- **Bonuses**: Night and weather surcharges
